About The Tutor Trees

Strong foundations. Clear branches.

A peer-to-peer tutoring community for students preparing for IGCSE, GCSE, AS/A Level and Pearson Edexcel examinations.

Read our story
The problem

Tutoring feels distant, and information is scattered.

Most tutors sat different exams a long time ago, and the timetables, syllabi and past papers a student needs live on five different sites.

What we do

Peers tutor peers, with everything in one place.

Students who recently sat these exact exams tutor the students preparing for them now, alongside the official timetables, syllabi and past papers, organised by level and board.

Why it matters

Nobody explains it like someone who just learned it.

A peer tutor remembers exactly which step was confusing and how they got past it. That, plus knowing what is examined and when, turns preparation into confidence.

    How it works

    Four steps from first message to exam-ready.

    The Tutor Trees pairs you with a student who has just been where you are, then keeps the whole preparation organised around your exact exam.

    1. Tell us your exam

      Level, board and subject: IGCSE, GCSE or AS/A Level, Cambridge or Pearson Edexcel. Everything downstream depends on this.

    2. Get matched with a peer tutor

      A student who recently sat the same papers. Pick from the tutor directory or send a booking request and we match you.

    3. Learn together, stay organised

      Sessions with your tutor, plus the official timetable, syllabus, past papers and a printable planner for your subject.

    4. Sit the exam ready, then pay it forward

      Walk in knowing the paper format and the mark scheme. Many students come back the next year as tutors. That is the tree.
